Help & getting started

Start with this month.

A few simple steps to get your budget and transactions in one place.

1. Set up your account

Create an account, then open Accounts to add a manual account for your imported transactions. If you already have an account, sign in here.

2. Bring in your transactions

In Transactions, choose Import transactions, select an account, and upload a CSV file. Match the date, description, and amount columns to your file. You can also map a credit/debit column when amounts are unsigned.

Download the CSV template for an example of the expected fields. Without a credit/debit column, use positive amounts for money in and negative amounts for money out.

3. Give your money a plan

Open Budgets and edit planned income to set your overall monthly income. Add budgets for your spending categories. Categories can be main categories or subcategories of an existing main category.

4. Check in as the month goes on

Compare recorded spending with your planned amounts. Open Other spending to see uncategorized transactions and spending in categories without a budget. Use the month timeline to review earlier periods.

A few useful details

What do the month colors mean?

A red month means visible spending exceeded recorded income for that period. Green means it did not. The comparison uses recorded income, rather than the income amount you planned.

Can I correct a transaction after importing it?

Yes. Use the edit control in Transactions to update its description, date, category, or notes. You can also hide a transaction so it is excluded from the budget totals.

What happens if I import the same CSV again?

Jaxon skips rows matching the same account, original date, description, and amount. Two separate purchases with exactly those same details can also match, so review your source file before importing.

Sign-in cookie

After sign-in, Jaxon sets a first-party cookie named session so the app can recognize your authenticated requests. It is used for account access, rather than advertising. The current sign-in session normally expires after ten days; signing out clears the cookie from that browser.

The cookie is marked HttpOnly so page scripts cannot read it. Its Secure setting depends on the deployment configuration and must be enabled for the live HTTPS service.

Theme preference

Jaxon uses local storage named theme to remember the light or dark display preference in your browser. It does not have an automatic expiry and remains until it is changed or cleared through your browser's site-data settings.

Your browser controls

You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Blocking the sign-in cookie can prevent authenticated features from working. Clearing theme storage resets the saved display preference.

External financial connections

If you open Plaid's account-linking flow, its technology and privacy disclosures also apply. Review Plaid's privacy information for its practices. This first-party inventory is not a complete audit of cookies or storage used by external providers.

Final storage and consent review

Before this notice takes effect, the live deployment must be checked for additional analytics, advertising, embedded tools, and provider storage.

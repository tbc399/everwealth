About Jaxon

A clearer picture.

Jaxon brings monthly budgets and everyday transactions together in one place.

Built around your month

Start with planned income, set amounts for the categories that matter to you, and compare your spending with your plan. Jaxon also groups spending outside those budgets, so it stays visible.

Your money, in context

Import transactions, organize them into main categories and subcategories, and look back at previous budget periods. The aim is simple: help you understand where your money goes and what is left for the month.
